On April 30 – May 1, 2026, we tabled at the University of Washington's Engineering Discovery Days, the College of Engineering's annual open house. We laid out our drone hardware — airframes, flight electronics, and boards — and walked visitors of all ages through how an autonomous UAV is designed, built, and flown.

Thanks to everyone who stopped by to talk drones with us.
